# Pre-registration: held-out swap suite (coordinate swap reproduction on items the settings were not tuned on)

Written 2026-10-17, **before any model has been run on the held-out items** (the generator and a fake-tokenizer test were run; no model saw the items).
`hlens/experiments/swap_heldout.py` records this file's SHA-256 and the suite's SHA-256 in its result file.

## Why
The swap reproduction (G2) has two weaknesses. (1) On Qwen3.5-4B the band and α were chosen by a sweep on the same 87 usable items they were then scored on (35 % at
band L12-20, α = 1.25), so the rate is not an unbiased estimate. (2) On Gemma 3 12B the pre-registered setting (band L18-30, α = 1.25) scored 8/48 (17 %, not passed),
and α = 1.0 reached 14/48 (29 %) only as a post-hoc look at the same items. A fresh suite lets both settings be tested without having been chosen on it.

## The suite
`data/experiments/probe-swap-heldout.json`, SHA-256 `003ae0cd00037d6873aa608f19d31fffdb33d10791251375ba3f99da70df5f12`, generated by `hlens/swap_suite.py` (seed 0) from fact tables:
**165 items** — city → country → {capital 32, language 30, continent 27, currency 34}, element → {symbol 18, state of matter 15}, language → country → capital 9. Same item format and
scoring as `probe-swap.json`. Constraints applied at generation: entities are single tokens (leading space) in **both** the Qwen3.5 and Gemma 3 tokenizers; answer and swap answer
differ in their first token under both; 24 candidates were dropped because their prompt or bridge city occurs in the original suite, 10 for multi-token entities.
**Baseline correctness is not a filter at generation**; each model's score is over its own baseline-correct items, as in G2.

## Protocol (identical to G2; nothing is tuned on the held-out suite)
bf16 model with its Neuronpedia lens (Qwen3.5-4B n = 417 fit; Gemma 3 12B `-pt`); swap the `intermediate` direction for `swap_to` at every token position, at every layer of the
band, one editor per layer, raw lens vectors, score the greedy next token at the last position against `swap_answer` (first-token match).
- **Band (depth-matched):** Qwen3.5-4B L12-20; Gemma 3 12B L18-30.
- **α:** **1.25 is primary** (for Gemma it was pre-registered in `preregistration_replication.md`; for Qwen it was chosen on the dev suite). **α = 1.0 is secondary** (it was the best α on
  the Gemma dev suite, post hoc; this is its first out-of-sample test). No other α is run in this pre-registration.
- **Controls:** three random-direction pairs per item (seeds 0, 1, 2) at the same band and α.

## Decision rule (per model and per α; the G2 rule)
**Pass iff** the number of baseline-correct items ≥ 30, **and** hit rate ≥ 25 %, **and** hits ≥ 5 × max(1, max over the three controls).
Also reported (not part of the rule): the Wilson 95 % interval of the hit rate; the rate by category family; the comparison with the dev-suite rate.

| Outcome | Statement |
|---|---|
| passes at α = 1.25 on both models | the swap reproduction generalizes to held-out items at the pre-registered setting on both architectures |
| passes on Qwen only | the swap reproduction is Qwen-specific at this setting; the Gemma 17 % on the dev suite is not an item-sampling accident |
| fails on Qwen at α = 1.25 | the 35 % dev-suite rate was inflated by tuning on the same items |
| passes on Gemma at α = 1.0 but not at 1.25 | the Gemma dev-suite α = 1.0 result was not a post-hoc accident, and the pre-registered α = 1.25 was the wrong setting; it is reported as such, not as a pass of the pre-registered setting |
| fewer than 30 baseline-correct items | not testable; the number is reported |

## Not part of this pre-registration
Re-running H1 (τ) and E1 (first-order margin vs nonlinear gain) on the held-out items — they need their own pre-registration; they would use the baseline-correct items of this suite.

## Known limits (stated in advance)
Templated relations are less varied than the hand-written paper items and are dominated by city → country questions (123 of 165); the facts (capitals, languages, currencies) are likely well
represented in pretraining, which affects baseline accuracy and may differ by model; "first-token match" scoring can credit a different word with the same first token (ambiguous items are
filtered at generation but only under the two tokenizers used); two α values; one band per model; items from the same relation share structure, so they are not independent (the Wilson interval
treats them as independent and is therefore optimistic).
